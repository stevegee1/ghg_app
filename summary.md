## 1. Executive Summary: What is this Platform?

**GHG Logo Drop** is an enterprise-grade B2B/B2C sustainability platform that solves two massive problems in the climate industry:

1. **Corporate Greenwashing & Unverified ESG Data**: Most corporate carbon reports rely on static annual spreadsheets and uncalibrated estimates. Our platform connects directly to **physical IoT/OT sensors** (data center PDU sub-meters, hydrogen electrolyzers, water flow meters), validates calibration certificates cryptographically, and locks report exports if emissions deviate by more than 15%.
2. **Disconnected Consumer Climate Action**: Instead of abstract carbon offsets, consumers and sports fans can immortalize memorable life milestones (such as winning derby goals, weddings, or marathon finishes) into **certified organic keepsake garments** embroidered with exact ISO 14067 Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) footprints and an automated, verifiable **15% ESG charitable split** routed to local nature restoration.

## 3. End-to-End Lifecycle: How it Works Step-by-Step

```
[ Facility OT Sensors ] ──▶ [ Calibration Check ] ──▶ [ Telemetry Ingestion ] ──▶ [ 15% Anomaly Engine ]
 (PDU / H2 / Water)           (SHA-256 Cert Hash)        (ISO 50001 / PUE)          (±15% Baseline Corridor)
                                                                                               │
                                                                                               ▼
[ Boardroom PDF Report ] ◀── [ Transparency Ledger ] ◀── [ Keepsake Drop ] ◀── [ 15% ESG Charity Split ]
  (SHA-256 Watermarked)        (Multi-Tenant RLS)         (Organic Embroidery)       (Direct Nature Funds)
```

### Step 1: Sensor Calibration & Gatekeeping (Milestone ISO-1)
* A partner facility (e.g., *Apex Hyperscale Data Centre*) registers its telemetry sensors (PDU meters, gas chromatographs).
* A valid **ISO 17025 Certificate of Calibration** must be uploaded with an unexpired date and SHA-256 hash.
* A native **PostgreSQL Database Trigger** physically blocks uncalibrated sensor data from ever entering the database (`HTTP 422 FAILED_PRECONDITION`).

### Step 2: Continuous Telemetry & 15% Baseline Anomaly Locking (Milestone ISO-2)
* Live telemetry is streamed into the platform.
* The system monitors metrics against calibrated baselines (e.g., nominal Data Centre PUE of `1.14`).
* If energy consumption shifts by **$\pm 15\%$**, the **Anomaly Lock Engine** immediately locks public report downloads (`HTTP 423 RESOURCE_LOCKED`) and logs an immutable non-conformance event under **ISO Clause 10.2**.

### Step 3: 5-Pillar ESG Risk Scoring & Speedometer Gauge (Milestone ISO-3)
* Companies complete weighted ISO 14065 audit questionnaires across 5 governance pillars.
* The system calculates a weighted score (`0.00` to `5.00`) and visually renders it on a **Risk Speedometer Dial**.
* If greenwashing is detected, the score drops to `0.00` and flags a critical compliance alert.

### Step 4: Custom Milestone Keepsake Studio (Milestone ISO-3)
* Consumers or corporate clients customize heavyweight organic garments (hoodies, caps) with personal triumph inscriptions (e.g., *"ARSENAL FC 2026 DERBY 89TH MIN GOAL"*).
* The studio embeds verifiable ISO 14067 LCA metrics (`520g CO2e`) and tracks the physical artisan embroidery labor hours.

### Step 5: Evidence Locker & Cryptographic PDF Report Generation (Milestone ISO-4)
* Facilities upload categorized audit evidence (SCADA telemetry, lab gas certs, water trade permits).
* Executives generate **Boardroom-Ready PDF Audit Reports** with streaming SHA-256 digital stamps and tamper-evident QR verification codes.

### Step 6: Legal Defense, Anti-Greenwash Linter & Dual-Licensing (Milestone ISO-5)
* **UK CMA Green Claims Code Linter**: Real-time copy checker highlights vague green claims (*"100% Green"*, *"Zero Emissions"*) and enforces verifiable terminology.
* **Exact 15% ESG Financial Split**: 15.00% of transaction volume is strictly routed to verified local environmental charities.
* **Section 106 Green Jobs**: Tracks skilled manufacturing and clean-tech employment hours.

## 5. Regulatory & Trademark Guardrails

1. **Nice Class 35 Software vs. Class 36 Brokerage Firewall**:
   - The platform provides data management, software assurance, and keepsake licensing under **Class 35**.
   - It strictly **does NOT broker carbon credits, trade offset futures, or take percentage brokerage fees**, keeping it 100% compliant with financial regulations.
2. **ISO 14065 Audit-Readiness Boundary**:
   - The platform is an *Audit-Readiness & Continuous Validation Engine*. It prepares and validates client telemetry for accredited external registrars (BSI, TÜV, DNV).
3. **UK CMA Green Claims Code**:
   - Built-in linguistic linter ensures all marketing and sustainability claims have direct empirical evidence before publication.
