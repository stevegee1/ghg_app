### Core Business Model & Regulatory Defense
1. **Management System Readiness vs. Statutory Registrar**: In accordance with **ISO 14065**, our platform acts as the continuous *Readiness, Telemetry Validation, and Chain-of-Custody Engine*. We prepare and protect the client’s data trails for external registrar certification (BSI, TÜV, DNV) and regulatory inspections (UK Environment Agency, Section 106 planning authorities).
2. **Class 35 vs. Class 36 Firewall**: Services are strictly defined as business data management and software assurance under Class 35. The platform does not broker carbon credits, trade carbon offsets, or take percentage-based brokerage on environmental attributes (avoiding unauthorized Class 36 financial brokerage).

# Milestone ISO-1: Class 35 Telemetry Ingestion & Mandatory Calibration Gatekeeper

### 1. Objective
Establish the audit-ready telemetry foundation. Transform the platform from storing unverified raw payloads into an administrative aggregator of calibrated Operational Technology (OT) telemetry. Enforce the **Mandatory Calibration Gatekeeper**: reject any telemetry stream if the corresponding physical sensor (power meter, water meter, gas chromatograph) lacks an active, unexpired, third-party Certificate of Calibration.

### 2. Backend & Data Engineering Deliverables
1. **Prisma Schema Migration**:
   - `AssetCalibrationRecord`: `id`, `licenseeId`, `assetTag`, `sensorType` (`ELECTRICITY_SUB_METER`, `WATER_FLOW_METER`, `GAS_CHROMATOGRAPH`, `THERMAL_FLOW_METER`), `calibrationDate`, `expiryDate`, `certDocumentUrl`, `certIntegrityHash` (SHA-256), `isCurrentlyValid`, `verifiedBy`.
   - `TelemetryLog`: `id`, `licenseeId`, `assetCalibrationId`, `metricType` (`PUE`, `WUE`, `MWH_LOAD`, `H2_PURITY_PCT`, `THERMAL_KW`), `numericValue`, `rawUnit`, `isWithinBaseline`, `requiresSupervisor`, `rawTelemetryJson`.
2. **PostgreSQL Safety Trigger (`enforce_active_calibration_on_telemetry`)**:
   - `BEFORE INSERT` trigger on `TelemetryLog` verifying that `assetCalibrationId` exists, `expiryDate >= NOW()`, and `isCurrentlyValid = true`. Rejects uncalibrated inserts at the database level with a standard SQL state exception.
3. **API Layer**:
   - `POST /api/iso/calibration/upload`: Authenticated endpoint allowing facility engineers to upload calibration certificates (PDF/PNG), auto-calculating document SHA-256 hash.
   - `GET /api/iso/calibration/assets`: RLS-scoped asset registry displaying calibration expiration countdowns.
   - `POST /api/iso/telemetry/ingest`: Secure server-to-server HMAC-authenticated endpoint for automated SCADA/BMS telemetry feeds.

### 3. Frontend Engineering & UI Deliverables (React + Vite)
1. **Asset Calibration Dashboard Component (`src/components/AssetCalibrationGrid.tsx`)**:
   - RLS-scoped tabular grid of all registered meters and sensors with sensor type icons.
   - Live calibration status badges: `Active (284 Days)` 🟢, `Expiring Soon (14 Days)` 🟡, `Expired / Locked` 🔴.
2. **Interactive Certificate Upload Dropzone Modal (`src/components/CalibrationUploadModal.tsx`)**:
   - Drag-and-drop file uploader accepting PDF and PNG calibration certificates.
   - **Client-Side SHA-256 Checksum**: Uses `window.crypto.subtle.digest('SHA-256', buffer)` to compute and display the checksum live in the UI before form submission.
   - Date pickers for Calibration Date and Expiry Date with instant client-side validation (`expiryDate > calibrationDate`).

### 4. Automated Test Suite (`tests/milestone-iso1.test.ts`)
- Test 1: Uploading a valid calibration certificate enables telemetry ingestion for that asset tag.
- Test 2: Ingesting telemetry for an uncalibrated or expired asset is rejected at the database trigger layer.
- Test 3: Raw telemetry payloads and cryptographic integrity hashes are stored immutably.
- Test 4: RLS multi-tenant isolation prevents Partner A from viewing Partner B's calibration assets.

# Milestone ISO-2: 15% Anomaly Lock Engine & Clause 10.2 Non-Conformance Tracker

### 1. Objective
Implement the automated operational risk controls required by **ISO 14001:2015 Clause 10.2** and **ISO 50001:2018 Clause 9.1**. If an asset metric drifts by more than **15%** outside established baseline models, automatically lock public data exports, issue a **"Pending Verification"** restriction, and generate a structured Non-Conformance Event requiring supervisor root-cause analysis.

### 2. Backend & Data Engineering Deliverables
1. **Prisma Schema Migration**:
   - `NonConformanceEvent`: `id`, `licenseeId`, `isoClause` (e.g. `ISO 50001:2018 Clause 9.1.1`), `assetTag`, `metricName`, `baselineValue`, `observedValue`, `percentageVariance`, `conformanceGrade` (`CONFORMANT`, `OFI`, `MINOR_NON_CONFORMANCE`, `MAJOR_NON_CONFORMANCE_GREENWASHING_RISK`), `rootCauseAnalysis`, `correctiveAction`, `assignedOwner`, `targetClosureDate`, `isResolved`, `signOffSupervisor`, `resolvedAt`.
2. **Variance Calculation & Export Lock Engine (`src/services/anomaly-detection.service.ts`)**:
   - Calculates dynamic rolling baselines for PUE, WUE, and hydrogen purity.
   - If $|\Delta| > 15\%$, sets `TelemetryLog.requiresSupervisor = true` and generates a `NonConformanceEvent`.
   - Restricts downstream endpoints (`/api/v1/transparency-ledger` and PDF certificate downloads) with `423 Locked: Pending Supervisor Verification`.
3. **Remediation & Supervisor Sign-off Workflow**:
   - `GET /api/iso/non-conformance`: RLS-filtered dashboard listing open non-conformance incidents.
   - `PATCH /api/iso/non-conformance/:id/resolve`: Supervisor endpoint requiring detailed root-cause documentation and corrective action plan before releasing the export lock.
   - Automated dispatch to `alertComplianceOfficer()` ([src/services/compliance.service.ts](file:///Users/mac/ghg_app/src/services/compliance.service.ts)) on major non-conformance detection.

### 3. Frontend Engineering & UI Deliverables (React + Vite)
1. **Telemetry Telemetry Waveform & Anomaly Corridor (`src/components/TelemetryWaveformChart.tsx`)**:
   - Visual telemetry time-series chart rendering the historical reading with a shaded **$\pm 15\%$ baseline corridor**.
   - Anomaly points exceeding 15% flash in bold amber/red markers.
2. **System Export Lock Banner (`src/components/ExportLockBanner.tsx`)**:
   - Prominent notification alert in the Partner Portal header: *"⚠️ System Export Lock Active: Metric variance of +17.5% detected on PDU-METER-04A. Transparency exports locked pending supervisor review."*
3. **Clause 10.2 Non-Conformance Remediation Drawer (`src/components/NonConformanceDrawer.tsx`)**:
   - Slide-over drawer displaying the incident timeline, observed vs baseline values, and Conformance Grading chip.
   - Integrated Markdown Editor for supervisors to input Root Cause Analysis, Corrective Action, and Target Closure Date.
   - One-click *"Sign Off & Release Lock"* action button (disabled unless user has `PARTNER` or `ADMIN` role).

### 4. Automated Test Suite (`tests/milestone-iso2.test.ts`)
- Test 1: Telemetry drift $>15\%$ locks transparency export endpoints with HTTP 423.
- Test 2: Telemetry within $\le 15\%$ baseline passes without locking.
- Test 3: Supervisor resolution with root-cause analysis unlocks the ledger and archives the audit entry.
- Test 4: Compliance alerts trigger immediately on `MAJOR_NON_CONFORMANCE_GREENWASHING_RISK`.

# Milestone ISO-3: Industrial, Event & Custom Milestone Keepsake Scoring Engine

### 1. Objective
Encode the master data-collection protocols, scoring matrices, and custom keepsake tracking across our primary industry verticals, event drops, and the **Custom Milestone Keepsake Studio**:
1. **Custom Milestone Keepsakes (Goals, Weddings, Triumphs)**:
   - Live 2D/3D composite canvas rendering personalized embroidered moments (*"Bukayo Saka 89' Top-Corner Winner"*, *"Sarah & Alex Wedding 2026"*).
   - Product Lifecycle Assessment (LCA) tracking under **ISO 14067** (100% organic cotton, zero-toxic dye, cradle-to-gate carbon metrics).
   - Direct integration with local artisan embroidery labor tracking (`JobCreationReport`).
2. **Data Centres & Microgrids**: PUE isolation (ISO/IEC 30134-2), cooling water loops (WUE), and community waste heat export (Section 106).
3. **Green Hydrogen & Heavy Industry (Steel DRI)**: 99.97% hydrogen purity (ISO 22734 / ISO 14687) and half-hourly renewable PPA matching.
4. **CleanTech Hardware Supplier Vetting**: Solar PV (IEC 61215), BESS cell-to-cell thermal runaway (IEC 62619), Heat Pumps (ISO 5149-2), and Cradle-to-Gate Embodied Carbon (ISO 14067).
5. **Commercial Green Fleet Procurement**: 5-Pillar weighted automotive scoring scorecard (High-Voltage Safety, Hydrogen Pressure Boundary, Fuel Purity, Cybersecurity ISO/SAE 21434, and Recyclability ISO 22628).

### 2. Backend & Data Engineering Deliverables
1. **Prisma Schema Models**:
   - `AuditInspectionReport`: `id`, `licenseeId`, `auditRefId`, `facilityType`, `leadAuditorName`, `evaluationDate`, `overallGrade`, `greenwashingRiskLevel`, `weightedScore`.
   - `AuditMatrixRow`: `id`, `reportId`, `pillarIndex`, `isoClauseLink`, `legalMandate`, `targetDepartment`, `questionText`, `objectiveEvidenceTypes`, `complianceGrading`, `rootCause`, `mandatoryCorrectiveAction`, `assignedOwner`, `targetClosureDate`, `verifiedBy`.
   - `CustomKeepsakeSpec`: `id`, `transactionId`, `garmentModel`, `inscribedText`, `threadColor`, `placement`, `artisanLaborHours`, `lcaCarbonGrams`.
2. **Weighted Scoring & Greenwashing Risk Calculator (`src/services/audit-scoring.service.ts`)**:
   - Computes weighted composite score:
     $$\text{Final Score} = \sum (\text{Pillar Score}_i \times \text{Weight}_i)$$
   - Categorizes Greenwashing Risk:
     - **Score 4.2 – 5.0**: `LOW_RISK_CONFORMANT` (Certified third-party data)
     - **Score 2.8 – 4.1**: `MEDIUM_RISK_OFI` (Opportunity for improvement)
     - **Score < 2.8**: `HIGH_RISK_GREENWASHING_EXPOSURE` (Vague data / missing proofs)
3. **API Endpoints**:
   - `POST /api/iso/audits/evaluate`: Submits audit questionnaire responses, computes weighted score, and persists matrix rows.
   - `GET /api/iso/audits/:id`: Fetches complete audit matrix with clause links and corrective actions.
   - `POST /api/custom-merch/preview`: Generates live canvas composite data for custom embroidery milestone text.

### 3. Frontend Engineering & UI Deliverables (React + Vite)
1. **Interactive Custom Milestone Garment Studio (`src/pages/CustomMerchStudioPage.tsx`)**:
   - Real-time **HTML5 Canvas Composite Studio**: Renders high-density embroidered lettering onto high-res blank apparel photos (caps, hoodies, vests, joggers) with realistic stitch textures, shadow drop, and placement coordinates (Left Chest, Right Sleeve, Back Collar).
   - Inscription Text Box with live character counter, thread color selector (Gold Metallic, Sterling Silver, Pure White, Emerald Green), and size/quantity matrix.
   - Live **Dual-Impact Preview Bar**: Displays the exact 15% charitable gift and estimated local artisan labor hours as the user configures their order.
2. **Interactive 5-Pillar Audit Wizard (`src/components/AuditMatrixWizard.tsx`)**:
   - Step-by-step audit interrogation UI with collapsible ISO clause explanations and legal mandate badges.
   - Compliance grading radio buttons: `Conformant (5)`, `OFI (3)`, `Minor NC (1)`, `Major NC / Greenwashing Risk (0)`.
   - **Dynamic Greenwashing Risk Speedometer (`src/components/RiskSpeedometer.tsx`)**: Animated circular dial updating in real time with color-coded risk bands.
